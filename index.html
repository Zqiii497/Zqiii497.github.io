# Zhuoqi He ### Reliable and Efficient Foundation Models [Email](mailto:zhuoqihe497@163.com) · [CV](cv.pdf) ## About I am an Algorithm Engineer at Noah Wellness Medical Clinic. I received my B.Eng. in Data Science and Big Data Technology from South China Agricultural University in 2025. Previously, I was a remote research intern at Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI). My research focuses on understanding and improving the reliability and efficiency of foundation models. I am particularly interested in multimodal knowledge editing, multilingual model quantization, and mechanistic interpretability. Through controlled experiments and interventions, I study how changes to model parameters and representations affect generalization and downstream behavior. ## Research **Multimodal Knowledge Editing** How well does an edited fact generalize across visual instances and reasoning tasks? I developed AKE-Bench to investigate visual generalization in multimodal knowledge editing and studied vision-side interventions through AK-EDIT. **Multilingual Model Quantization** How does low-bit quantization affect different languages? I investigate language-dependent performance degradation and use layer- and neuron-level interventions to understand its underlying mechanisms. **Dialogue Summarization** How can dialogue structure and global topics improve summarization? I developed MLEGT, a model that combines utterance representations, keyword and speaker graphs, and a topic-guided pointer mechanism. ## Publications **Multi-level Encoder with Global Topic for Task-oriented Dialogue Summarization** **Zhuoqi He**, Peijie Huang, Yuhong Xu, Youming Peng, Mingzhi Xu, Xinyang Lin *IEEE International Conference on Acoustics, Speech and Signal Processing (ICASSP), 2025.* **A Multi-Intent Fusion Framework for Joint Intent Detection and Slot Filling** Shangjian Yin, Peijie Huang, Dongzhu Liang, **Zhuoqi He**, Qianer Li, Yuhong Xu *Chinese National Conference on Computational Linguistics (CCL), 2022.* ## Research Manuscripts **Does Knowledge Editing Systematically Generalize in Multimodal Large Language Models?** **Zhuoqi He**, Zhaoyi Li, Dylan Xinming Hou, Daniil Ognev, Muhammad Asif Ali, Gus Xia, Lijie Hu *Manuscript, 2026.* **Understanding Quantization-Induced Performance Degeneration in Multilingual Large Language Models** **Zhuoqi He**, Zhaoyi Li, Yi Lu, Di Wang, Lijie Hu *Manuscript, 2026.* ## Experience **Noah Wellness Medical Clinic** Algorithm Engineer · May 2026–Present Developing personalized dynamic baselines from longitudinal user data and building a requirement-to-purchase pipeline for personalized product recommendations. **Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI)** Remote Research Intern · June 2025–April 2026 Led two first-author studies on multimodal knowledge editing and multilingual model quantization, including benchmark design, experimental evaluation, and analysis of model behavior. **South China Agricultural University** Undergraduate Researcher · October 2023–May 2024 Developed a structure- and topic-aware dialogue summarization model and conducted ablation studies and human evaluation.
